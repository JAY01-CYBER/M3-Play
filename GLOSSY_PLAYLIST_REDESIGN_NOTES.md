# Glossy-inspired playlist redesign

Updated `OnlinePlaylistScreen.kt` to replace the centered square artwork and four-action card with a full-width hero artwork, palette fade, title overlay, and three-control Shuffle/Play/Add row. The existing playlist endpoints and menu actions are reused. The top app bar is transparent when expanded.

This is a layout port adapted to M3 Play's models, not a pixel-perfect copy of Glossy's entire app. Build and device verification are still required.
