M3-Play SoundCloud + JioSaavn integration update

Implemented in this source package:
- Player & Audio settings now has a separate Music source selector: YouTube Music, SoundCloud, JioSaavn.
- Online search reads the selected source and searches SoundCloud tracks or JioSaavn tracks.
- External results are converted to M3-Play SongItem/MediaItem metadata and queued through ListQueue.
- MusicService detects SoundCloud URLs and JioSaavn track IDs and resolves them through the matching provider instead of sending them to the YouTube resolver.
- SoundCloud playback request headers and stream markers are handled by the media OkHttp interceptor.
- SoundCloud progressive transcodings are preferred to match M3-Play's progressive MediaSource path.
- Fixed duplicate SoundCloudAudioQuality/JioSaavnAudioQuality enum declarations: definitions remain in ExternalAudioQualities.kt.

How to select:
Settings > Player & Audio > Music source > choose YouTube Music / SoundCloud / JioSaavn.
Then open Online Search and search a track. Existing YouTube stream client selection remains a separate setting and applies only to YouTube.
